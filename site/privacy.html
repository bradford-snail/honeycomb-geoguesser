Privacy Policy

Last updated: [Month Day, Year]

Honeycomb Geoguesser (“the game”) is operated by [Company name] (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the game collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. If you have questions, contact us at [contact email].

What we collect

  • Your leaderboard name. The name you choose before playing. It is shown publicly on the leaderboard.
  • Gameplay information. Your scores, the speed you played, where you placed your pins, how many games you’ve played, and when you played.
  • An anonymous player ID. When you first visit, the game creates a random ID so it can remember your name and scores between visits. It is not linked to your real identity.
  • Technical information. Like any website, when your browser loads the game, our service providers receive standard technical information such as your IP address and browser type. They may keep it in their logs for security and operation.

What we don’t collect

We don’t ask for your email address, real name, phone number, payment details, or your device’s location. The game has no advertising and uses no advertising or tracking cookies.

How we use it

  • To run the game and show your results.
  • To display public leaderboards.
  • To keep the game fair, for example by limiting how many games can be started in an hour, and to remove inappropriate names.

We don’t sell your information or share it with anyone for marketing.

Information stored in your browser

The game saves your anonymous player ID and your preferred screenshot speed in your browser’s local storage. This is needed for the game to work. If you clear your browser data, the game will treat you as a new player and you won’t be able to reclaim your previous name or scores.

Service providers

We use these providers to run the game. They process information on our behalf or under their own privacy policies:

  • Supabase: database and file storage (names, scores, game data, images). Data is stored in [region, e.g. the United States].
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the website.
  • Google Fonts: provides the typeface used on the site.
  • jsDelivr: delivers a code library the game uses.

How long we keep it

We keep your leaderboard name and gameplay information for as long as the game is running, unless you ask us to delete it or we remove it under our Terms. Leaderboards may be reset from time to time. Our service providers keep their technical logs according to their own policies.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to show you, correct, or delete the information connected to your player by contacting [contact email] with your leaderboard name. Depending on where you live, for example in the European Union, the United Kingdom, or California, you may have additional rights under local law, and you can contact us to exercise them.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect personal information from them. Please don’t use your real name or any other personal information as your leaderboard name. If you are a parent and believe your child has shared personal information, contact us and we’ll remove it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the game changes. We’ll update the date at the top when we do.
